Quick Checkout

TCGBot's own button for your normal browser. ⚡ Buy sets your quantity, adds to bag, goes through checkout (details → delivery → payment) filling in what you've saved here, and stops at the final button — you press "Checkout securely". It only acts when you press a TCGBot button.

Option 1 · TCGBot browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera)

  1. Go to chrome://extensions in your browser's address bar (Edge: edge://extensions).
  2. Turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked and choose this folder:
  3. Set the Qty and press ⚡ Buy next to a product below: it opens the product in a new tab and runs Add to Bag → Checkout automatically. (Or open a product and press Add to Bag → Checkout in the panel bottom-right.)

Option 2 · Bookmark button (Safari, Firefox, any browser)

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Safari: View → Show Favourites Bar).
  2. Drag this button onto the bar: ⚡ TCGBot Checkout
  3. On a product page (Open ↗ or ⚡ Buy below), click the bookmark: it sets your quantity, adds to bag and goes to checkout. If the store shows the bag page instead, click it once more there.

The bookmark carries your list as it is right now — drag it onto the bar again after you change the list.

      Activity

        Getting started

        1. Profile & payment: enter your shipping details (and optionally a card).
        2. Settings → Retailer logins: open each store in the bot browser and sign in once.
        3. + Add item: pick a preset or enter a link / SKU / keywords and choose the stores.
        4. Press ▶ Start monitoring. Leave this tab open for sound and desktop alerts.

        What happens when something drops

        The status changes to In stock or Pre-order open and you get an alert. With Checkout assist the bot browser jumps to the product, adds it to the cart, goes through checkout filling in your details and stops on the final Place order / Pay now button (highlighted in green). You review and click it.

        The ⚡ Go button on any item does the same thing immediately, e.g. when you know a drop is live.

        Things the bot leaves to you

        • CAPTCHAs, "are you human" checks and queues: it pauses, alerts you, and continues after you complete them.
        • Logging in, bank verification (3-D Secure), and the final purchase click.
        • Retailer purchase limits are respected: one account, the quantity you set.

        Keywords

        one piece, op-18|op18, booster box|display means: must contain "one piece" AND ("op-18" OR "op18") AND ("booster box" OR "display"). Accents, case and dashes don't matter ("op-18" = "OP18"), but "op-1" won't match "op-18". Use Exclude for accessories (sleeves, playmats…). Use Preview matches to check what a store returns.

        Statuses

        • Not listed yet keyword/SKU watch: no matching product on that store yet. It keeps searching.
        • Coming soon listed but not orderable.
        • Blocked by site the store refused the request. "Auto" switches to the browser window; otherwise increase the interval.
