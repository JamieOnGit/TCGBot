Questions

Which stores does it work with?

The extension works on the big Australian stores that sell trading cards, including Kmart, EB Games, Zing, JB Hi-Fi, Target, BIG W, Toys"R"Us and Amazon AU. Restock alerts work with most product pages. Stores change their sites from time to time, and some block automated stock checks. When that happens we'll tell you in your dashboard.

Does it buy things automatically?

No. It gets you to a filled-in checkout and highlights the final button. You review your order and press it yourself, every time.

Is my card safe?

Your card is never sent to our servers. You can save it in the browser extension, encrypted with a passphrase only you know. Once you unlock it, the extension can type it into the store's own payment form. Your delivery details are stored encrypted and tied to your account only.

Can other people see my details or lists?

No. Every list, address, watchlist, alert setting and activity entry belongs to your login alone. Our support staff can see your email and plan, not your details.

Does it work on my phone?

One-click checkout needs the extension in a Chromium browser on a computer. Restock alerts reach any device through Discord or email.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from Billing in your account. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Is this allowed?

Every store has its own terms of use, and it's up to you to follow them. {{APP_NAME}} keeps you in control: one account, the store's limits, no CAPTCHA or queue skipping, and you place every order yourself.
