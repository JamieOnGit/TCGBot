Add a product

Set the quantity, then press ⚡ Buy. A new tab opens the product, and the extension adds it to your bag, goes to checkout and fills in your details. You press the final button.

    Watchlist

    Get an alert as soon as a product is in stock or open for pre-order. Each page is checked about every 60 seconds.

      Delivery details

      Used by the extension to fill in checkout. Encrypted, and only for your account. Anything a store already filled in from your store account is left as it is.

      Payment card

      Your card is never stored on our servers. Save it in the extension's Settings instead: it's kept in that browser only, encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and tied to your account. Unlock it when you're ready to buy, and checkout fills it in for you.
